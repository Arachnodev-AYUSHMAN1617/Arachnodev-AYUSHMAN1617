## `$ whoami`

A frontend developer from **Chhattisgarh** who accidentally became full-stack three months into his first job.

The backend said *hold my coffee*. I said *okay but I am going to audit you first.*

I own the Angular 21 frontend of a live industrial e-auction platform and built a B2B metal trading marketplace from zero to 85% shipped, solo, in under two months. At night I work on [**Easygarhiya**](https://github.com/Arachnodev-AYUSHMAN1617/Easygarhiya) so that Gondi language and Chhattisgarhi culture do not quietly disappear while the rest of the internet argues about tabs vs spaces.

6 months in. Already doing full-stack. The learning curve was vertical. Zero complaints.

## The Chhattisgarh Corner

Forest cover: 44 percent. Chhattisgarh is more jungle than state.
Languages: Chhattisgarhi. Gondi. Halbi. None of them on Wikipedia properly.
Art: Dhokra casting. Bastar ironwork. Warli painting.
The problem: A severe shortage of developers who give a damn about any of this.


I am building **Easygarhiya** to change that. Multilingual. Culturally accurate. The same care I put into production-grade frontend, applied to things that actually matter.

**Jai Johar.** That is a traditional Gondi greeting. It means: peace be with you.
